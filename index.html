01Ollama Cloud

Sync with your 01

Your phone uses Ollama Cloud for replies. When your computer’s 01 is connected, chats and settings also sync with it. It cannot control your computer.

Ollama Cloud handles phone replies. If your computer’s 01 is offline, the phone simply cannot sync until it comes back.

Sync includes conversations and preferences only. The mobile app never has mouse, keyboard, screen, app, file, or command access on your PC.
